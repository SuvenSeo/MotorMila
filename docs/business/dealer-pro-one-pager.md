# MotorMila for Dealers

**Price your stock right. Move it faster.**

MotorMila tracks Sri Lanka's vehicle market every day, by model, year, mileage and district. The Dealer workspace shows you where each car on your lot sits against the market, and sends you buyers.

## What you get
- **Price check on your own stock:** each car compared with the market median and range for the same model, year and mileage band.
- **Aging and price-gap alerts:** see which cars have sat too long and how far they are above market.
- **Buyer leads:** leasing and inspection requests from MotorMila buyers looking at your cars.
- **Verified dealer badge** on your listings (Pro and Yard).
- **Team seats** for your sales staff (Yard).

Market comparisons are statistics (medians and ranges). We never show you other sellers' personal details.
