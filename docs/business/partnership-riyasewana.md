# Draft: partnership proposal to Riyasewana

**To:** Riyasewana Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, via the contact page on riyasewana.com (or the showroom / advertising sales contact)
**From:** Suven Seoras, founder, MotorMila
**Subject:** Partner proposal: sending buyers to Riyasewana ads from MotorMila

---

Dear Riyasewana team,

I'm the founder of MotorMila (motormila.vercel.app), a Sri Lankan vehicle price-comparison site. Buyers use it to check whether an asking price is fair, using medians by model, year and district and import-cost maths.

Riyasewana is one of the most important sources of vehicle ads in the country, and I'd like MotorMila to work with you under a written agreement.

**Our proposal**
- **Traffic back to you:** every Riyasewana listing on MotorMila is a short summary card that links to the ad on riyasewana.com. All links carry UTM tags (`utm_source=motormila&utm_medium=referral`), and we'll share a monthly click report.
- **Attribution:** a "Riyasewana" source label on every card. We'll never present your ads as MotorMila's own.
- **Photos and phones:** we won't copy, rehost or alter your watermarked images, and we won't show seller phone numbers. Buyers call sellers through Riyasewana. We never touch `/get-phone.php`.
- **Takedown on request:** removal of any ad within 24 hours of your request, and full deletion of Riyasewana data within 7 days if we stop working together.
- **Honest access:** a named user agent, at a crawl rate and schedule you approve, or a feed if you prefer.

**Commercial ideas**
- A share of revenue from leasing and inspection requests that start on a Riyasewana ad. Riyasewana already runs leasing partners, so we could route those leads to **your** leasing partners instead of ours.
- A paid "Showroom on MotorMila" add-on for your showroom members.

Could we arrange a short call or a meeting in Colombo?

Kind regards,
Suven Seoras
