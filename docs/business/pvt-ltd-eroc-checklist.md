# Checklist: register a private limited company in Sri Lanka (eROC)

Under the Companies Act No. 7 of 2007, through the Registrar of Companies' online system, eROC (https://eroc.drc.gov.lk). **Check current fees and forms on the DRC site. They change.** Not legal advice. A company secretary or lawyer can do all of this for a fixed fee.

## Before you start
- [ ] Pick 2–3 name options (e.g. "MotorMila (Private) Limited"). The name must end with "(Private) Limited" / "(Pvt) Ltd".
- [ ] At least **1 director** (18+) and at least **1 shareholder**. Can be the same person.
- [ ] A **company secretary**: a qualified individual or a registered secretarial firm. Usually hired. Expect an annual retainer.
- [ ] Registered office address in Sri Lanka.
- [ ] NIC (or passport) copies for every director and shareholder, plus email and mobile number.
- [ ] Decide the shares: number issued and the stated capital (it can be small).

## On eROC
1. [ ] Create an eROC user account and verify your email.
2. [ ] **Name search, then name reservation.** Pay the reservation fee. The reservation is valid for a limited period.
3. [ ] Fill in the incorporation forms online:
   - [ ] **Form 1:** application for incorporation (company details, shares, directors, shareholders, secretary).
   - [ ] **Form 18:** consent and certificate of each director.
   - [ ] **Form 19:** consent and certificate of the secretary.
   - [ ] **Articles of Association:** use the standard model articles, or upload your own.
4. [ ] Upload documents and have them signed (directors sign as eROC asks).
5. [ ] Pay the registration fee online.
6. [ ] Receive the **Certificate of Incorporation** (PV number) by email, usually within a few working days.

## After incorporation
- [ ] **Public notice** of incorporation, if eROC / the Act requires it for your case (check the DRC guidance).
- [ ] **Company seal / rubber stamp**, if you want one (optional under the Act).
- [ ] **Bank account** in the company's name (HNB, DFCC, Cargills Bank). Bring the certificate, Form 1, articles, a board resolution and directors' NICs.
- [ ] **Tax registration (TIN)** with the Inland Revenue Department (IRD e-Services / RAMIS). Check VAT and SSCL thresholds.
- [ ] **EPF / ETF** registration once you hire employees.
- [ ] Local-authority business / trade licence for the office address, if required.
- [ ] Share certificates issued to shareholders. Keep the share register and minute book.
- [ ] **Annual return (Form 15)** every year, and audited financial statements as the Act requires.

## Move MotorMila into the company
- [ ] Assign IP (code, brand, domain) from you to the company in a short written assignment.
- [ ] Move domains (.lk via LK Domain Registry), Google Play developer account, Vercel / Hugging Face / Neon / Cloudflare billing, and the PayHere merchant account into the company's name.
- [ ] Apply for **PayHere** as the company (business registration documents are needed for Plus/Premium and recurring billing).
- [ ] Update the legal pages: replace `[COMPANY NAME] (Pvt) Ltd, PV [REG. NO.]` in `src/components/money/LegalPageShell.tsx`.
- [ ] Set up the privacy@ mailbox and name a DPO (PDPA Part III, from 1 Jan 2027).
- [ ] Consider basic cyber / professional-liability insurance.
