# Draft: partnership proposal to ikman.lk

**To:** partnerships / business development, Saltside Technologies (ikman.lk). Find the right contact on LinkedIn ("ikman partnerships" or "Head of Vehicles"), or use the ikman business contact form.
**From:** Suven Seoras, founder, MotorMila
**Subject:** Sending ikman vehicle buyers from MotorMila: partner-site proposal

---

Hi [NAME],

I run MotorMila (motormila.vercel.app), a Sri Lankan site that helps buyers check whether a vehicle's asking price is fair: medians by model, year and district, price trends, and import-duty maths.

Many of the ads our users compare are ikman ads. Before we grow further, I'd like to do this properly with you rather than around you. ikman's terms already allow advertisers' content to be shown on "ikman's partner sites", so I'd like to propose that MotorMila becomes one.

**What we'd do**
1. **Send every click to the ad on ikman.** Each listing on MotorMila is a short card (make, model, year, price, mileage, district) with a "View on ikman" button. No seller phone numbers on MotorMila. Buyers contact sellers on ikman.
2. **UTM-tag all traffic** (`utm_source=motormila&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=listing`), so you can measure it in your analytics. We'll also send a monthly report of clicks per category.
3. **Clear attribution:** an "ikman.lk" source label on every card and a line on every listing page saying the ad belongs to the seller and ikman.
4. **Photos on your terms:** none, or one thumbnail loaded from your CDN with credit. Watermarks untouched. Whatever you prefer.
5. **Takedown on request:** any ad or seller you ask us to remove is gone within 24 hours, and we drop all ikman data within 7 days if the partnership ends.

**What we'd ask for**
- A feed (XML/JSON, or a sitemap plus agreed crawl rate) of live vehicle ads with the fields above, or written permission to read public ad pages at a rate you set, with a named user agent.

**Commercial options** (happy to discuss)
- Revenue share on leasing and inspection leads that start from an ikman ad on MotorMila, **or**
- A "Featured on MotorMila" add-on you can sell to your members alongside Bump Up and Top Ad, with MotorMila paid per placement.

We're small, so this is low risk to try. I'd suggest a 3-month pilot on cars only, with a review at the end.

Could we have a 30-minute call in the next two weeks?
