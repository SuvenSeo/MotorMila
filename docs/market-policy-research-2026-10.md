# Sri Lanka vehicle market: policy, regulatory & data opportunities (as of 2026-10-03)

Research-only. Compiled from 4 parallel research passes + verification searches. Every figure carries [source, date]. **UNVERIFIED** flags mark claims without primary-source confirmation.

## Five premise corrections

1. Private-car imports reopened **1 February 2025**, not September 2025 (gazettes 2421/04 and 2421/44, 27–31 Jan 2025).
2. There is **no government permit-auction scheme** — only a gray-market permit trade (permitsale.lk, Rs 3.6M–22M by category, 16 May 2026) plus Customs auctions of seized/abandoned vehicles (1,025 units, Hambantota, July 2026).
3. "Open general licence" is **not a live legal instrument** for vehicles 2025–26; imports run on gazette regulations under the Imports and Exports (Control) Act 1969.
4. "CTI / Ceylinco Transit Inspection" does not exist as named — the real regimes are the **Vehicle Emission Test (VET)** (LAUGFS Eco Sri + CleanCo/DriveGreen) and commercial fitness certification.
5. The "2025 hybrid battery/kW tax change" did **not** happen as described. Hybrids are excised per engine cc at a ~20–25% lower table than petrol; EVs moved to per-kW excise (roughly doubled), and battery/motor power became a *classification* test (HEV/PHEV/MHEV/REEV/BEV).

## 1. Import policy 2020–2026

**Timeline.** LCs suspended 19 Mar 2020; three-stage reopening approved 13 Sep 2024 (ft.lk): transport Oct 2024, goods Dec 2024, **personal vehicles 1 Feb 2025** (Regs 01 & 02 of 2025).

**Current regime (Oct 2026).** Registered importers: unlimited quantity, no FX ceiling. Everyone else: one vehicle per 12 months. Age limits: cars/SUVs/motorcycles **≤3 years** (5-year relaxation refused for Budget 2026 — sundaytimes.lk 2025-10-19). Anti-hoarding: register within 90 days or 3% of CIF/month late fee capped at 45%; importers must register ≥25% of each six-month batch. Concessionary-duty permits barred for these HS codes. Bureau Veritas sole pre-shipment verification (Gazette 2488/07). Tyre standards SLS 1212/1347 from 1 Jan 2027 (Gazette 2508/27).

**Duty stack HS 8703.** (1) CID 20% → **30% base from 1 Apr 2026** (Gazette 2478/03). (2) **50% surcharge on the CID line** (not on vehicle value — common misreading): imposed Feb 2025–Feb 2026, **reimposed 16 May 2026** (2488/56), **extended to 31 Dec 2026** (2501/88, 13 Aug 2026). Effective CID today = **45% of CIF**. LCs ≤15 May 2026 exempt only if on board by 15 Nov 2026 and unamended (2488/69). (3) Excise per-cc for ICE/hybrids, per-kW for EVs/REEVs. (4) Luxury Tax 60–120% of CIF above Rs 5.0M/5.5M/6.0M thresholds (petrol/hybrid/EV — Gazette 2421/41). (5) PAL/CESS exempt per most calculators — **conflicting** (induwara.lk charges PAL 10%); verify against Ch. 87 tariff before coding. (6) SSCL 2.5% moved to import point from ~April 2026 (Budget 2026) and enters VAT base. (7) VAT 18% cascading. (Vehicle Emission Levy flat Rs 15,000; NBT dormant.)

**Customs valuation.** Gazette 1971/10: used = ≥85% of transacted value of similar new vehicle in export country + freight/insurance. The **15% used-vehicle valuation reduction is in contradictory state**: VIASL said removed June 2026 (newswire.lk 2026-06-17), CMTA said Sept 2026 it still applied (ft.lk 2026-09-11), Court of Appeal case pending. **Do not hard-code without a customs circular.**

**Permits.** Budget 2026: "There will be no permits." IMF MEFP (CR 26/111) commits to keeping suspension and reviewing cancellation of existing permits (~0.6% of GDP) in the **2027 Budget**. Closed diaspora EV permit scheme (1,077 permits) under COPA/AG scrutiny.

**Price reactions.** Ban era: used prices 2–3× pre-ban. Oct 2025 forced selling (Vezel Z Play Rs 25.5M→23.5M, Yaris 11.5→10.5M, Alto Hybrid 7.9→7.3M). May 2026 surcharge sparked a rise wave. Sept 2026: down Rs 0.4–1M on oversupply. Registrations: May peak 62,776 → Aug 45,811 (−13.9% MoM); hybrids took 49% of new SUV registrations in Aug (25% in July), EV share fell 28%→14% (ft.lk 2026-10-01). Customs' 2026 vehicle-tax estimate Rs 266bn, −69.4% YoY.

## 2. Hybrid/EV taxation & buyer search demand

**Hybrids** = per-cc excise, lower table (Gazette 2418/43): ≤1,000cc Rs 1,810,900/unit; 1,001–1,300 Rs 2,750/cc; 1,301–1,500 Rs 3,450/cc; up to Rs 12,050/cc. Petrol equivalents ~20–25% higher. Classification (Mar 2025): hybrid must use both ICE and motor for *mechanical propulsion*; mild-hybrids taxed as petrol. **REEVs (e-POWER) are excised per kW like BEVs** (Gazette 2434/04).

**EVs** = per-kW excise, age-tiered, roughly doubled 31 Jan 2025 (Gazette 2421/42): ≤50kW Rs 18,100/kW (≤12mo) / 36,200 / 48,300; up to >200kW Rs 96,600 / 132,800 / 144,900. The 50% CID surcharge covers hybrids **and** BEVs. Enforcement flashpoint: ~1,000 BYD Atto 3s detained July 2025 over kW declarations.

**Search demand.** ~15 duty calculators compete (cal.lk powers ikman's CarsGuide tool; kapruka, calclk, induwara, pixamp, lankawebsites, wahana, welandapola, firstcar, pricemart, tariff.lk which ships an **MCP server for AI clients**, sltaxcalculator…). **Quality is the opening**: tariff.lk's EV line wrong, firstcar.lk wrong on EV excise, welandapola shows surcharge inactive (wrong — runs to 31 Dec 2026), lankawebsites FX feed 5+ months stale. Top content angles: hybrid-tax myth; "50% surcharge" misread as +50% price (actually +15% of CIF); SSCL change misread as new tax; motor kW vs battery kWh; PHEV/REEV classification. **No Sinhala-language duty calculator exists — genuine gap.**

## 3. RMV ownership transfer

Process per dmt.gov.lk: MTA 6 (4 copies) + MTA 8; file at DMT transfer branch (Colombo 05/Narahenpita, Werahera, district offices; 9am–2pm weekdays); 2 photos, NIC, revenue licence, CR/GR, lease-deletion letter if mortgaged, GN/JP no-objection. One-day service at Narahenpita/Werahera only.

**Fees** (Gazette 2306/02, Nov 2022): car registration Rs 5,000 normal / 6,500 priority / 7,500 one-day; plus **provincial stamp duty ~1% of declared value** (the big number), plate charges on province change, first-transfer special tax Rs 3,000–4,000 (varies), 14-day filing deadline. **UNVERIFIED: late-penalty formula** (per-day Rs 100 vs one-off — resolve Regulation 6(1)(b) before shipping an estimator).

**Digitization nuance.** "e-Motoring" overhaul stalled ("silent mode" Aug 2026; COPA sent 25 corruption cases to AG Mar 2026). Working today: eRL 2.0, **DMT paid vehicle-info lookups** (owner name/address, engine no., liens), DMT Smart App, appointment portal, a **mandatory online transfer channel introduced 2026** (biometric verification at Divisional Secretariat). Customs **Vehicle Verification Portal** by chassis number (May 2025, post-2005). DMT "Digital Vehicle Information Sheet" (2026) replaces CR/GR with QR/smart-card record incl. liens — trade-press sourced, **UNVERIFIED**; if real, future rails for vehicle history.

**Pain points.** Chassis/engine tampering, illegal swaps, forged lease-clearance letters, "open papers" liability, runner handling quoted 2–5% of sale price (single source), notary Rs 1,000–5,000.

## 4. Insurance, inspection, financing

**Insurance.** Compulsory third-party has statutory minimum tariff (e.g. 1,001–1,500cc car = Rs 3,275/yr incl. stamp). Comprehensive detariffed, industry indicator 1.8–3.5% of insured value (mid ~2.6%). **2026 shifts**: premium credit capped 30 days (Direction 5/2026, from 1 May); **digital-only insurance cards** (physical banned 1 May 2026); leasing-company insurance leads **only through registered brokers from 1 Oct 2026** (Direction 4/2026) — reshapes any embedded quote flow. Aggregators exist (InsureMe, ClicktoInsure, PolicyHub, Araksha, Motorbazee) but **no dominant neutral live-quote aggregator**.

**Inspection.** Private cars: no periodic roadworthiness — only annual VET (Rs 1,690 car, VAT-incl. from 1 Oct 2025); visual check includes chassis/engine number reading (fraud checkpoint). BEVs exempt; hybrid exemption contested (UNVERIFIED). **Motor Vehicles (Inspection) Order No. 03 of 2026** (Gazette 2489/22) mandates roadworthiness inspection for passenger/transport vehicles — Cabinet approved 4 Aug 2026, Parliament approval pending. VET operator contracts expire end-2026 (re-tender pending). <15% of 8.1M vehicles currently face any mandatory test.

**Financing — the cycle reversed.** CBSL hiked OPR +100bps to **8.75%** (26 May 2026), held 29 Sep. **LTV caps tightened from 25 May 2026** (Directions 01/2026): cars/SUVs/vans/three-wheelers **max LTV 40%** (was 50%), commercial 60%, vehicles registered >1 year 60% — used buyers need ≥40% down. Rates: banks ~11.5–13.0%, NBFIs ~13.0–14.5% (People's Leasing risk-based 12–40%). **No dominant national leasing marketplace** (IdeaBeam, Easy Leasing app, TaprobaneFi fragmented). UNVERIFIED: claimed "18% AER lease ceiling".

## 5. Currency angle

Duty computed at **Customs "Rates of Exchange"** — weekly gazetted schedule (50 currencies), distinct from market rates. Verified: USD 336.1284 / JPY 2.1522 (21–27 Sep 2026, Gazette 2507/01). May 2026 scandal: Customs froze rate at Rs 351.17 while market recovered (ft.lk 2026-05-27). LKR: ~310 (Dec 2025) → spike ~350 (21 May 2026) → ~330.6 (3 Oct 2026); rupee −6.2% YTD. **The surcharge may expire 31 Dec 2026 if the rupee holds** (The Morning, 2026-09-06) — the single biggest live unknown for any calculator. Remittance rules tightened (Gazette 2493/39).

## 6. Language & channel behavior

DataReportal 2026: 13.9M internet users (59.7%), 30.3M mobile connections. TRCSL Q4 2025: WhatsApp 16.54M, Facebook 16.67M, Viber 10.87M. Similarweb SL Play Store Sep 2026: WhatsApp #1, **WhatsApp Business #2** in communication. **APIDM/Kelaniya Digital Outlook 2026 (n=2,948, Jul–Aug 2026): 92% smartphone ownership; 81% of internet users use AI tools for information discovery, growing numbers bypassing traditional search** — SEO-only acquisition is decaying faster in SL than most markets. No reliable Sinhala/Tamil vs English browsing stat (UNVERIFIED); Census 2012: 87% Sinhala, 28.5% Tamil, 23.8% English. Listings close "call or WhatsApp 077…" — chat-native commerce. WhatsApp Business API ecosystem real (go4whatsup: Sinhala+Tamil+English on one number, LankaQR payment links) but no published adoption stat.

## 7. Local fintech/marketplace innovation

- patpat.lk most finance-integrated (budget search + leasing apply); CARMART.lk × HNB Leasing bundle (Feb 2026).
- **Government registry layer is purchasable**: DMT paid lookups + Customs chassis portal. Commercial wrappers exist (carregistrationapi.lk plate→13 fields API; RegCheck SL endpoint incl. owner loan status). **"Vehicle history lite" is shippable today at per-lookup cost, zero government partnership.**
- **Zero commercial Carfax-equivalent** (three academic blockchain prototypes cite data silos as the blocker). MyMechanic.lk pre-purchase inspections; Auto+ garage software (per-chassis service records — potential data partner).
- EV charging: chargeNET dominant; Volt Charge aggregating networks + OCPI 2.2.1 hub (Jul 2026) — roaming layer forming.
- **No consumer "scan plate → price + history" product exists**; induwara ships a plate-format decoder + API; combining Motormila's app camera with plate decode → FMV is shippable from public data alone.

## Search demand: own vs table stakes

| Surface | Competition today | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Duty calculators | ~15 tools + ikman's; 4+ demonstrably wrong/stale | **Table stakes.** Ship a gazette-watched correct one (auto-alerts on rate changes, weekly Customs FX, LC-date-aware surcharge) to beat the field |
| Permit/policy trackers | None as a product | **Ownable** — "what did this gazette change about your watchlist's landed cost" |
| Transfer-fee estimators | induwara built one | Weak alone; strong as one line in a "total deal cost" card (price + transfer + stamp + VET + insurance) |
| Insurance quotes | 5 aggregators, no dominant neutral | Partner-dependent (broker-only rule for leasing leads since 1 Oct 2026) |
| Leasing comparisons | Fragmented; **ikman could build it in a sprint** | Model LTV caps explicitly; B2B2C risk-pricing via district velocity is the deeper play |
| Price-drop alerts | riyasewana app, adfinder, LesiSearch | **Defensible via cross-site dedup** — real drop vs re-list artifact |
| FMV / fair price | ikman seller-side (coarse), PriceMart (2-source medians) | **Strongest moat**: 275K cross-site deduped listings with price history |
| Vehicle history | DMT lookups purchasable; zero commercial products | Ask-price history + re-list detection unique; accident/service history needs partnerships |

**Moats and risks.** Moat = cross-marketplace deduped price history (nobody else can or will do cross-site identity), district velocity, gazette-linked price impact. Risks: (1) ikman owns demand and ships adjacent tools — it can crush calculators; (2) the 81%-AI-search shift cuts both ways: SEO moats decay but structured citable data gets *quoted* by assistants (tariff.lk's MCP server is the pattern to copy); (3) chat-native commerce means the real product surface is WhatsApp-delivered alerts/summaries; (4) policy churn is a content engine but every calculator is one gazette from being wrong — gazette-watching infrastructure *is* the product.

**Live unknowns to watch**: surcharge expiry 31 Dec 2026 (December review), 15% used-vehicle valuation reduction (contradictory), e-Motoring rollout, VET re-tender (end-2026), Inspection Order 03/2026 approval, 2027 Budget permit cancellation. Freshest sources run to 2026-10-03.
